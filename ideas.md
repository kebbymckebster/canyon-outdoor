## Chosen Direction — Pacific Monolith

### Design Movement

**Pacific Monolith** draws from contemporary Californian residential architecture, editorial property-development design, and late-modernist wayfinding systems. The website is intentionally cinematic without leaning into novelty or construction-site tropes.

### Core Principles

1. **Scale with restraint:** Oversized uppercase editorial headlines carry the emotional weight, while supporting content remains concise and precisely measured.
2. **Material contrast:** Deep charcoal, warm limestone, and muted sand create a tactile field for earthy imagery and carefully placed forest-green accents.
3. **Architecture before ornament:** Dividers, labels, coordinates, and fine rules are treated as the visual language; decorative plant illustration and generic landscaping iconography are avoided.
4. **Deliberate reveal:** Sections unfold like a guided site walk with layered photos, restrained motion, and tactile interaction rather than constant animation.

### Color Philosophy

The foundation is **obsidian charcoal**, establishing authority and making warm Southern California sunlight feel richer. **Limestone and shell** provide a quiet, gallery-like canvas. **Dry sand and weathered clay** convey native materiality, while a highly controlled **canyon green** acts as a spatial signal—not a conventional lawn-service color.

### Layout Paradigm

The page follows a **processional landscape**: full-bleed visual thresholds alternate with editorial rooms of content. Rather than centering every message in uniform bands, elements align to shifting left-hand site lines, wide image fields, and inset utility rails that mimic an architectural drawing set.

### Signature Elements

1. A fine **topographic contour motif** appears in hero overlays, concept panels, and selected background surfaces.
2. **Coordinate labels and mono-style specification lines** add a credible planning-led language throughout the experience.
3. **Rounded-square navigation markers** and circular cursor-like sliders reference material samples and site pins.

### Interaction Philosophy

Interactions should feel closer to navigating an architectural proposal than playing with a dashboard. Hovering reveals plan notes, sliders invite direct comparison, and configurator selections change the conceptual atmosphere immediately. Every response must have a clear, material-feeling cue.

### Animation

Motion is slow, cinematic, and surface-led: imagery has restrained parallax, reveal masks move with a soft custom easing, and controls give brief tactile feedback. Content transitions favor opacity and transform only. Motion is reduced or removed for visitors who request reduced motion.

### Typography System

**Space Grotesk** provides the primary headline voice: assertive, contemporary, and architectural in uppercase. **Manrope** handles navigation, explanatory text, and form controls with a calm readable rhythm. Labels use Manrope in small, tracked uppercase; large headlines use tight Space Grotesk with intentional line breaks.

### Brand Essence

**Canyon Outdoor is a Southern California design studio turning overlooked exterior areas into considered, visualized outdoor environments.**

**Personality:** Architectural, grounded, progressive.

### Brand Voice

The voice is compact, assured, and landscape-literate. Headlines should be declarative, while calls to action feel invitational but direct.

> “Start with the site. Build toward the life around it.”

> “Bring the view into the plan.”

### Wordmark & Logo

The wordmark is an expanded uppercase **CANYON / OUTDOOR** lockup with a small offset horizon rule. The icon is a simple carved **C** formed by two nested, interrupted contour lines—strong enough to work alone as a favicon, marker, or navigation emblem.

### Signature Brand Color

**Canyon Green — #29433A**

## Style Decisions

- The **CANYON / OUTDOOR** lockup, horizon rule, and carved contour-C are recurring navigation, section-marker, concept-panel, and footer signatures—not occasional decoration.
- Capability, environmental, and process content is treated as a field of **architectural specification panels** with site rails, datum lines, and coordinate labels, rather than generic marketing cards.
- Visual pacing alternates between quiet limestone proposal rooms and immersive charcoal/image thresholds. Photography is distributed across wide exterior views, shadowed hardscape, and planting/material detail so no single outdoor scene carries the whole experience.
